Launched in 2021, this pioneering undergraduate program is designed to equip students with strong fundamentals in engineering alongside cutting-edge computational skills. Through a carefully structured curriculum and an emphasis on hands-on problem solving, our students are well-prepared to address modern industrial challenges.

We foster a culture of creativity, collaboration, and critical thinking. Our students are not only technically sound but also research-oriented and ready to make meaningful contributions from day one.
